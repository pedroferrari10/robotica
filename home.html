O que é Robótica?

Robotics is an educational and technological branch that deals with systems composed of automatic mechanical parts together with integrated circuits, creating motorized mechanical systems controlled by electrical circuits and computational intelligence. Robotics is an object of study in various fields: computer science, aerospace, mechanics, automation, electrical engineering, etc.

Aplicações da robótica

As more and more robots are designed for specific tasks, this classification method becomes more relevant. For example, many robots are designed for assembly work, which may not be easily adaptable to other applications. They are called "assembly robots." For seam welding, some suppliers provide complete welding systems with the robot—that is, the welding equipment along with other material handling facilities, such as rotary tables, as an integrated unit. Such an integrated robotic system is called a "welding robot," even though its discrete manipulator unit could be adapted to a variety of tasks. Some robots are designed specifically for heavy load handling and are labeled as "heavy-duty robots."

Causa e efeito:

More and more people are using robots for their tasks, such as vacuum cleaning robots and medical surgery robots. This technology, now adopted by many factories and industries, has generally succeeded in areas such as cost reduction, increased productivity, and the reduction of labor issues. However, despite the advantages, robots end up bringing other specific problems, such as the dismissal of numerous human employees.
